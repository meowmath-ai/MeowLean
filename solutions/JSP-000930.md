# JSP-000930

Let `m` be a cardinal with ℵ₀ < `m` < 𝔠. If a family of entire functions has at most `m` distinct values at each complex point, must the family have cardinality at most `m`? This is Hayman's Problem 2.46, listed as Erdős Problem 1119. The Justin Sun Prize catalog records independence from ZFC. [Original statement](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1119) · [Catalog entry](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/main/problems/catalog-0901-1000.md#JSP-000930)

The main Lean theorem is `JSP000930.Consistency.hayman_1119_relative_independence`. It gives relative consistency for the quantified Hayman sentence and its literal negation. Both theories include 𝔠 = ℵ₂. In these models the admissible cardinal bound is `m = ℵ₁`. [Main theorem](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/JSP000930RelativeIndependence.lean)

The sentence quantifies over ordinal representatives of the cardinal bound. At every complex point it forms the exact image of the family under evaluation, requires an injection from that image into the bound, and concludes that the whole family injects into the same bound. It represents complex numbers by pairs of Dedekind cuts and entire functions by Taylor series with infinite radius of convergence. The analytic proofs relate these codes to entire functions and identify the set-language evaluation relation with the Taylor limit in the Boolean-valued models. [Cardinal and family quantifiers](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/JSP000930WetzelSentence.lean) · [Set-language sentence](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/JSP000930ActualHaymanSentence.lean) · [Entire-function correspondence](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/JSP000930AnalyticTaylor.lean) · [Evaluation audit](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/AuditJSP000930LiteralEval.lean)

The positive direction uses a Cohen model and separates functions by their values at a fresh generic point. The negative direction uses a finite-support iteration to construct a family of size ℵ₂ whose evaluation image at each point has size at most ℵ₁. [Positive model](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/JSP000930PositiveClosedPositiveCohenModel.lean) · [Negative model](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/JSP000930RelativeIndependence.lean) · [Refutation of the sentence](../supplements/JSP-000930/src/JSP000930NegativeLiteralHaymanRefutation.lean)

## Reproduction

The repository pins Lean and Mathlib 4.36.0. With Git, Elan, and Python 3.10 or newer installed, run the following from the repository root.

```sh
lake exe cache get Mathlib
lake env python supplements/JSP-000930/check.py
```

The replay recompiles the proof and checks the diagnostics from the syntax, evaluation, positive-model, and independence audits.
